Method Statement for Excavation and Backfilling

Method statement template for excavation and backfilling — service detection, shoring, spoil management, PPE and emergency arrangements.

Scope of works

Excavation to formation level, disposal or reuse of spoil, and backfilling in compacted layers.

Sequence of works

#Activity
1Identify and mark underground services before breaking ground.
2Establish exclusion zones and safe access/egress.
3Excavate in sequence with shoring or battering as designed.
4Keep spoil and plant back from the excavation edge.
5Inspect the excavation daily and after weather events.
6Backfill in layers and compact to specification.

Plant, equipment and materials

  • Excavator
  • Dumper or conveyor
  • Compaction plate / roller
  • Shoring or trench boxes
  • Cable avoidance tool (CAT)

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Hard hat
  • Safety boots
  • Hi-vis vest
  • Gloves
  • Dust mask (as required)
  • Ear protection

Hazards and control measures

HazardControl measures
Underground servicesCAT scan, trial holes, permit to dig
Excavation collapseShoring, battering, daily inspection, no unsupervised entry
Plant / pedestrianSeparated routes, banksmen, exclusion zones
Dust and noiseWater suppression, PPE, permitted working hours

Emergency arrangements

Evacuate and secure the area; call emergency services for any trapped person; never attempt an unauthorised rescue. Report to the site supervisor immediately.
