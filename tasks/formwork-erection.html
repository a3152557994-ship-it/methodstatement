Method Statement for Formwork Erection

Method statement template for formwork erection — falsework design, assembly, striking, inspection and fall protection.

Scope of works

Erection, inspection, and striking of wall and slab formwork and its falsework support system.

Sequence of works

#Activity
1Confirm formwork and falsework design and load capacity.
2Set out the formwork layout and mark positions.
3Erect falsework and props on stable base, level and brace as designed.
4Assemble panels, align and fix; apply release agent.
5Inspect formwork and supports before the pour.
6Strike formwork after the specified strength is reached, in reverse order.

Plant, equipment and materials

  • Formwork panels and accessories
  • Props, jacks and falsework
  • Hand tools and hoists
  • Release agent and applicator
  • Access towers

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Hard hat
  • Safety boots
  • Gloves
  • Hi-vis vest
  • Safety glasses
  • Harness (where required)

Hazards and control measures

HazardControl measures
Formwork collapseApproved design, inspection, no premature loading
Working at heightPlatforms, guardrails, harness where required
Falling objectsExclusion zone below, tool tethering
Manual handlingTeam lifts, hoists, planned handling

Emergency arrangements

Evacuate the area and secure it in the event of movement or collapse; call emergency services for any injury.
