Method Statement for Plumbing Rough-in

Method statement template for plumbing rough-in — pipework, pressure testing, drainage, PPE and controls.

Scope of works

Installation of first-fix pipework, drainage and supports, including pressure testing and protection.

Sequence of works

#Activity
1Confirm the layout and check for clashes with other services.
2Install supports and pipework to the correct fall and spacing.
3Cut and join pipework using the approved method.
4Pressure test and record results before closing up.
5Protect open ends and make safe; cap drains.
6Update as-built records.

Plant, equipment and materials

  • Pipe cutting and threading tools
  • Press or solder equipment
  • Pressure test pump
  • Supports and brackets
  • Access equipment

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Safety boots
  • Gloves
  • Safety glasses
  • Hard hat
  • Hi-vis vest

Hazards and control measures

HazardControl measures
Hot work / solderingPermit, fire watch, extinguisher
Water damageIsolate and test, controlled drainage
Manual handlingTeam lifts, supports
Sharp edgesGloves, correct tools

Emergency arrangements

For burns cool the area and seek first aid; for leaks isolate the supply and inform the site supervisor.
