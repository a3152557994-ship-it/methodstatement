Method Statement for Tiling Works

Method statement template for tiling works — substrates, adhesives, cutting, water damage prevention and hazards.

Scope of works

Preparation and tiling of walls and floors to the specified layout and standards.

Sequence of works

#Activity
1Check substrate condition, flatness and moisture before starting.
2Plan the layout and set out datum lines.
3Prime the substrate and apply adhesive to the correct notch and coverage.
4Fix tiles cleanly with spacers, checking level and alignment.
5Cut and drill tiles safely; keep wiring routes clear.
6Grout, clean and inspect against the specified tolerances.

Plant, equipment and materials

  • Tile cutter and angle grinder
  • Adhesive mixer
  • Notched trowels, levellers and spacers
  • Wet saw
  • Cleaning tools

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Safety boots
  • Gloves
  • Safety glasses
  • Knee pads
  • Dust mask (as required)

Hazards and control measures

HazardControl measures
Silica dustWet cutting or extraction, dust mask
Cuts from tools and tilesGloves, eye protection, guards
Manual handlingTeam lifts, trolleys
Kneeling injuriesKnee pads, rotation of tasks

Emergency arrangements

For eye injuries irrigate immediately and seek first aid; report dust exposure incidents.
