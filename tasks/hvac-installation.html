Method Statement for HVAC Installation

Method statement for HVAC installation — rigging, ductwork, refrigerant safety, testing and controls.

Scope of works

Installation of ductwork, plant and refrigerant pipework to the approved design.

Sequence of works

#Activity
1Confirm design, plant locations and safe access.
2Rig and set plant using rated lifting equipment and exclusion zone.
3Install supports, ductwork and pipework to specification.
4Brazing/brazing only under hot work permit with fire watch.
5Pressure test, evacuate and charge refrigerant to design.
6Commission, record and hand over.

Plant, equipment and materials

  • Lifting equipment and slings
  • Ductwork and supports
  • Pipe benders and brazing kit
  • Vacuum pump and gauges
  • Access platforms

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Hard hat
  • Safety boots
  • Gloves
  • Safety glasses
  • Hi-vis vest
  • Respirators (as required)

Hazards and control measures

HazardControl measures
Lifting operationsRated equipment, planned lift, exclusion zone
RefrigerantTrained persons, PPE, ventilation, no venting
Hot workPermit, fire watch, extinguisher
Working at heightPlatforms and guardrails

Emergency arrangements

For refrigerant exposure move to fresh air and seek medical attention; for fire follow hot work emergency plan.
