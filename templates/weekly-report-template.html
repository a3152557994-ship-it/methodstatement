Weekly Report Template

A free construction weekly report template — progress, look-ahead, issues, safety and photos for clients and directors.

What to include

  • Progress this week vs programme
  • Look-ahead for next week
  • Labour/plant/resources
  • Quality and safety observations
  • Risks, delays and mitigation
  • Photos and approvals

How to use

Copy the headings into your document or spreadsheet, fill in the blanks on site, and keep it with the daily records. Consistent records are what protect both the project and the people on it.
