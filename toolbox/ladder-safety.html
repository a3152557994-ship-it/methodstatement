Toolbox Talk: Ladder Safety

A 5-minute briefing for the whole gang. Read it out, ask the questions, record attendance.

Key points

  • Check ladders before use — stiles, rungs, feet.
  • Set at 1 in 4 angle and tie off at the top.
  • 3 points of contact, keep body within stiles.
  • Do not carry loads that affect balance — use a hoist or bag.
  • No work from the top two rungs.
  • Ladders are for access and short work — use a platform for long tasks.

Ask the crew

  • Where on this site could this hazard affect you today?
  • What control are you relying on — and is it in place?
  • Who do you tell if it isn't?
